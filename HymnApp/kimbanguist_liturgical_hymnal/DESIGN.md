## Brand & Style

The design system is crafted for a sacred, contemplative, and functional digital hymnal serving the Igreja Kimbanguista community. It unites the serene digital clarity of the modern reference aesthetic with the sacred heritage of Kimbanguism—grounded in purity, hope, and worship.

The design movement is **Sacred Minimalism with Tactile Warmth**:
- **Purity & Breathability:** Generous off-white surfaces (`#F9F9F9`) evoking ceremonial vestments and sanctuary stillness.
- **Liturgical Reverence:** Anchored by deep ecclesiastical green paired with symbolic warm gold accents, complemented by serene sky blue for digital utility and audio feedback.
- **Distraction-Free Focus:** High-contrast, deeply legible typography tailored for worship environments ranging from sunlit open assemblies to dim candlelight vigils.
- **Tactile Musical Touch:** Floating audio bars, scripture strophes, and hymn number badges designed to feel like refined devotional bookmaking transposed into a modern mobile experience.

## Colors

The color palette bridges the symbolic spiritual colors of Kimbanguism—deep liturgical forest green and sacred gold—with the refined, clean functional tokens from the design base (`#F9F9F9`, `#4A90E2`, `#111111`, `#666666`).

- **Primary (`#1B4D3E`):** Sacred Forest Green. Symbolizes spiritual endurance, hope, and fellowship. Used for primary CTAs, top navigation headers, active navigation pills, and stanza verse indicators.
- **Secondary (`#C59B27`):** Liturgical Gold. Represents divine illumination and choral reverence. Reserved for chorus markers, favorites star activation, golden badge accents, and celebration modes.
- **Tertiary (`#4A90E2`):** Sanctuary Sky Blue (from reference). Dedicated to musical utility: playback scrubbing, audio track timestamps, pitch indicators, and audio drawer actions.
- **Neutral (`#111111`):** Deep Obsidian. Delivers crisp, uncompromised contrast for hymn lyrics and reading matter against pure surfaces.
- **Functional Neutrals & Accents:**
  - `sacred-surface` (`#F9F9F9`): Soft, non-glare background for reading comfort during long services.
  - `text-muted` (`#666666`): Standard secondary text for tune credits, scripture references, and meters.
  - `kimbangu-green-soft` (`#E8F0EC`): Container fill for badges, hymn number cards, and active stanza highlights.

## Typography

The typographical strategy pairs the geometric, modern authority of **Montserrat** with the ecclesiastical, dignified rhythm of **Noto Serif**.

- **Hymn Reading & Strophes:** Lyric text uses `hymn-verse` (Noto Serif 19px/32px), calibrated intentionally for high scanability when standing or singing in congregation. Chorus passages adopt `hymn-chorus` (Noto Serif Medium Italic) with a slight left indent and subtle tint bar.
- **Devotional Numbers & UI Controls:** `Montserrat` serves all functional UI typography: hymn index numbers, navigation bars, search fields, metadata (meter, key signature, composer, tone), and audio tracking chips.
- **Reading Adjustments:** Stanza numbering utilizes `label-numeric` in deep green or gold, providing instant visual markers during rapid page scrolling.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is built around a single-column fluid mobile canvas transitioning to a balanced centered reader on tablets:
- **Mobile (<600px):** 4-column fluid layout with `1.25rem` (20px) outer margins. Song lyrics span full width with generous `1.5rem` vertical spacing between stanzas to prevent accidental visual line-jumping during worship.
- **Tablet / Expanded (601px - 1024px):** Fixed-width centered reading column (max width 680px) flanked by liturgical utility rails for the audio player, song parts (soprano, alto, tenor, bass), and cross-referenced scripture.
- **Safe Footers:** Audio player dock introduces an automatic `5rem` bottom padding buffer across all scrollable song sheets, ensuring last verses are never occluded.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids heavy commercial dropshadows in favor of **Tonal Layering and Sacred Ambient Diffusions**:

1. **Surface Base (Level 0):** Flat `#F9F9F9` background canvas.
2. **Card & Verse Tiles (Level 1):** Solid `#FFFFFF` surfaces with subtle hairline borders (`1px solid rgba(17, 17, 17, 0.06)`).
3. **Floating Audio Control / Bottom Sheet (Level 2):** Elevated via an ultra-soft, warm ambient shadow: `0 8px 32px rgba(27, 77, 62, 0.08)` coupled with an ultra-light golden blur highlight at the top border (`1px solid rgba(197, 155, 39, 0.2)`).
4. **Modals & Key Selectors (Level 3):** Centered dialogs with dark green-tinted backdrop scrim (`rgba(17, 24, 20, 0.5)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(6px)`).

## Shapes

The design system implements **Roundedness 2 (0.5rem / 8px standard)**. 

- Standard interactive components (buttons, input boxes, hymn card list items) feature `0.5rem` corner radius, balancing contemporary cleanliness with approachable comfort.
- Hymn number identity badges and quick-jump category tags feature soft pill contours (`rounded-full` / 9999px) to echo songbook tab markers.
- Sheet presentation containers and audio playback trays employ smooth top radiuses (`1.25rem` / 20px) for a natural nesting feel.

## Components

### Hymn List Item & Quick Cards
- **Structure:** Left-aligned rounded badge containing the hymn number in `Montserrat Bold`, followed by a title stack (Kikongo/Lingala/French/Portuguese titles) and meter/theme label.
- **Right Action:** Quick favorite toggle star (unfilled `#666666`, active `#C59B27`).
- **Interaction:** Micro-scale on press with soft background transition to `#E8F0EC`.

### Persistent Worship Audio Player
- **Design:** Compact floating bar docked above bottom system navigation.
- **Visuals:** Surface colored `#FFFFFF` with primary green play/pause glyph, dynamic song duration scrubber tinted with `#4A90E2`, and pitch/tempo adjust drawer toggle.
- **Controls:** Play, Pause, 10s Rewind, Loop Chorus, and Vocal Part switcher.

### Search & Numerical Dial Bar
- **Fast-Filter:** Search input field featuring dual-mode input (Instant numeric jumper keypad button + full-text lyric/title query input).
- **Styling:** `#FFFFFF` background, `1px solid rgba(17, 17, 17, 0.12)`, left-placed magnifying icon in `#1B4D3E`.

### Stanza & Chorus Typography Blocks
- **Verses:** Numbered with a soft green numeral prefix, standard text color `#111111`.
- **Chorus / Refrain:** Indented `1rem` with a subtle vertical left golden accent border (`2px solid #C59B27`) and italicized `Noto Serif` rendering to signal choral participation instantly.

### Buttons & Liturgical Chips
- **Primary Button:** Filled `#1B4D3E` with crisp `#FFFFFF` text, `0.5rem` border radius, high-touch target height (48px).
- **Secondary Button:** Outlined `1.5px solid #1B4D3E` with `#1B4D3E` text on transparent or white background.
- **Filter Chips:** Categorical tags (e.g., "Nkembo", "Mawete", "Pasika", "Favorites") styled with light green background `#E8F0EC` and deep green text, shifting to solid gold `#C59B27` when marked as favorite filter.
